About

Hi, I'm Jebun.

I'm a PhD candidate at UNIST (IRIS Lab) working at the intersection of AI and atmospheric science. My research improves methane monitoring end to end: correcting satellite retrieval bias, fusing multiple sensors, filling the gaps, and feeding better data into emission inversion. I also work on ammonia (NH₃) and ozone (O₃).

Beyond my PhD, I'm deeply interested in machine learning and data science in general. In my free time I build projects on weather forecasting, agentic AI, foundation models and business data.

PhD research

Better methane monitoring, from satellite to emissions.

Each step uses machine learning to make the next one more reliable.

Retrieval bias correctionLearn and remove systematic errors in each satellite sensor's methane retrievals.
Multi-sensor fusionHarmonize several sensors and merge them into one consistent record. Published 2026
Gap-fillingDeep learning on spatial and temporal context to fill what clouds and orbits miss.
Improved inversionFeed better observations into inverse models for more accurate emission estimates.
Also working onAmmonia (NH₃)Ozone (O₃)
How I work

From a raw dataset to something people can use.

ExploreUnderstand the data: coverage, gaps, outliers and what the target really is.
EngineerClean, join and build features, and keep test data untouched.
ModelStart with a strong baseline, then try deep models where they earn their cost.
ValidateHold out whole groups, places or periods so scores reflect real generalization.
ShipCode, a clear README, figures and, where it helps, an interactive demo.

CH₄
Atmospheric Measurement Techniques · 2026 · first author

Enhanced methane monitoring: a globally harmonized daily 0.1° XCH₄ through machine learning-based fusion of GOSAT, GOSAT-2, and TROPOMI

J. N. Keya, Y. Kim, H. Choi, J. Im

Gradient-boosted models bias-correct three satellite data streams against ground truth and fuse them into one global daily dataset. R² 0.81, RMSE 10.78 ppb at withheld sites.
