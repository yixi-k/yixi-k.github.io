Biography

Hi, I'm Jebun, a PhD candidate at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST), South Korea, working in machine learning, data science and AI. I enjoy the full loop: exploring a new dataset, choosing the right model, and validating it so the results hold up on data the model has never seen.

My PhD research applies machine learning and deep learning to large-scale atmospheric data. Outside of it, I build hands-on projects across data science, computer vision, forecasting, agentic AI, foundation models and quantitative trading. This website collects that work.
