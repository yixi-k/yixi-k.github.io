About

Hi, I'm Jebun.

I'm a PhD candidate in Environmental Engineering at UNIST, South Korea, in the Intelligent Remote Sensing and Geospatial Science (IRIS) Lab. I build machine learning and deep learning models for satellite Earth observation.

My main line of work is global methane mapping: reducing the uncertainty in satellite XCH₄ so that emissions can be tracked with confidence. In parallel I work on precipitation nowcasting for early warning, on ammonia and ozone, and I experiment with agentic AI.

Outside Earth observation, I'm into data science in general. For fun, I pick up datasets from fields far from my own and build small projects around them. You'll find them below.

Research

Machine learning for data that is biased, sparse and full of gaps.

Satellites see the atmosphere unevenly: clouds, orbits and sensor biases leave holes and errors. My models correct, combine and complete those observations.

Main line · published + ongoing

Global methane mapping

Bias correction and fusion of several satellite data streams into one consistent global daily XCH₄ dataset, and spatio-temporal deep learning that fills the remaining gaps.

Approach

From raw retrievals to a product people can trust.

CollocateMatch satellite data with ground-truth stations in space and time.
CorrectLearn and remove each sensor's systematic bias, with SHAP to check what the model relies on.
FuseHarmonize sources to one reference and merge them into a single record.
FillDeep learning on spatial and temporal context fills cloud and orbit gaps.
ValidateHold out whole sites, months and years so scores reflect real generalization.

Enhanced methane monitoring: a globally harmonized daily 0.1° XCH₄ through machine learning-based fusion of GOSAT, GOSAT-2, and TROPOMI

J. N. Keya, Y. Kim, H. Choi, J. Im
