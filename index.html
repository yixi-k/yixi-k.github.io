I am a researcher at the Intelligent Remote sensing and geospatial Information Science (IRIS) Lab, UNIST, advised by Prof. Jungho Im. My research combines satellite remote sensing and machine learning to monitor atmospheric methane (CH₄).

I built a globally harmonized, daily 0.1° XCH₄ product by bias-correcting GOSAT, GOSAT-2, and TROPOMI against TCCON ground stations, harmonizing them to a common reference, and fusing them into one consistent record (2020–2023). The paper is published in Atmospheric Measurement Techniques (2026), and the dataset is openly available on Zenodo.

I also work on agentic AI for urban flood decision support and on training data for a geostationary satellite foundation model.

Research interests: greenhouse-gas remote sensing · multi-sensor data fusion · agentic AI for Earth observation · geospatial foundation models.
