I am a TODO-position at the [Intelligent Remote sensing and geospatial Information Science (IRIS) Lab](https://iris.unist.ac.kr/), UNIST, advised by Prof. Jungho Im.
My research combines **satellite remote sensing** and **machine learning** to monitor atmospheric **methane (CH₄)**.

I built a globally harmonized, daily 0.1° XCH₄ product by bias-correcting GOSAT, GOSAT-2, and TROPOMI against TCCON ground stations, harmonizing them to a common reference, and fusing them into one consistent record (2020–2023).
The paper is published in _Atmospheric Measurement Techniques_ (2026), and the dataset is openly available on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20304047).

I also work on **agentic AI** for urban flood decision support and on training data for a **geostationary satellite foundation model**.

**Research interests:** greenhouse-gas remote sensing · multi-sensor data fusion · agentic AI for Earth observation · geospatial foundation models.
